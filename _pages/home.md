### Welcome!

I am an integrative global change ecologist, and my research focuses on the causes and consequences of species losses in terrestrial ecosystems. Global change is causing rapid, large-scale species declines and extirpations

I earned by PhD from Brown University in 2023, working with Dr. Tyler Kartzinel to quantify the impacts of megaherbivore exclusion on plant and animal communities in a Kenyan savanna. I am currently a postdoc at the University of California Santa Barbara, where I investigate the impacts of megacarcasses (>1,000 kg; elephants, rhinos) on savanna ecosystem processes in the Kruger National Park, South Africa. 

Megacarcass Ecology Working Group: megacarcassecology.com
